Comprehensive Information on Different Crops

Grain Crops

Crop Name: Rice

Varieties: BRRI Dhan-28, BRRI Dhan-29, Basmati.
Cultivation Method: Direct seeding or transplanting.
Soil Requirement: Clay or loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Harvest when grains turn golden; hand or machine.
Nutrition Value: Carbohydrates, protein, B vitamins.
Potential Uses: Food, rice flour, animal feed.

Crop Name: Wheat

Varieties: Shatabdi, BARI Gom-33, Sonalika.
Cultivation Method: Direct seed sowing.
Soil Requirement: Well-drained loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Harvest when golden and dry.
Nutrition Value: Carbohydrates, protein, fiber.
Potential Uses: Flour, bread, pasta, animal feed.

Crop Name: Corn (Maize)

Varieties: Sweet corn, Dent corn, Flint corn.
Cultivation Method: Direct seed planting in rows.
Soil Requirement: Fertile, well-drained loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Harvest mature ears manually or mechanically.
Nutrition Value: Carbohydrates, fiber, vitamin B.
Potential Uses: Food, livestock feed, biofuel.

Fruit Crops

Crop Name: Mango

Varieties: Langra, Himsagar, Amrupali.
Cultivation Method: Plant grafted saplings.
Soil Requirement: Deep, well-drained loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Hand-pick ripe fruits.
Nutrition Value: Vitamins A & C, fiber.
Potential Uses: Fresh fruit, juice, jam, pickles.

Crop Name: Banana

Varieties: Cavendish, Sabri, Amritasaga.
Cultivation Method: Plant suckers or tissue culture plants.
Soil Requirement: Fertile, well-drained loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Cut mature bunches by hand.
Nutrition Value: Potassium, vitamin B6, fiber.
Potential Uses: Fresh fruit, chips, desserts.

Crop Name: Apple

Varieties: Fuji, Gala, Red Delicious.
Cultivation Method: Plant grafted saplings.
Soil Requirement: Well-drained loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Hand-pick mature fruits.
Nutrition Value: Fiber, vitamin C, antioxidants.
Potential Uses: Fresh fruit, juice, pies, cider.

Vegetable Crops

Crop Name: Tomato

Varieties: Roma, Cherry, Beefsteak.
Cultivation Method: Transplant seedlings.
Soil Requirement: Fertile, well-drained soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Fertile, well-drained soil.
Nutrition Value: Vitamin C, vitamin A, lycopene.
Potential Uses: Salads, sauces, ketchup.

Crop Name: Potato

Varieties: Cardinal, Diamant, Granola.
Cultivation Method: Cardinal, Diamant, Granola.
Soil Requirement: Loose sandy loam soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Dig out mature tubers.
Nutrition Value: Carbohydrates, potassium, vitamin C.
Potential Uses: Food, chips, fries, starch.

Crop Name: Carrot

Varieties: Nantes, Kuroda, Imperator.
Cultivation Method: Direct seed sowing.
Soil Requirement: Loose sandy loam soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Pull mature roots by hand.
Nutrition Value: Vitamin A, fiber, antioxidants.
Potential Uses: Salads, juice, cooking.

Cash Crops

Crop Name: Tea

Varieties: Assam, Chinese, Cambod.
Cultivation Method: Plant seedlings or cuttings.
Soil Requirement: Acidic, well-drained soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Pluck young leaves by hand.
Nutrition Value: Antioxidants, polyphenols.
Potential Uses: Tea beverage, herbal products.

Crop Name: Coffee

Varieties: Arabica, Robusta, Liberica.
Cultivation Method: Plant seedlings.
Soil Requirement: Well-drained fertile loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Hand-pick ripe coffee cherries.
Nutrition Value: Antioxidants, caffeine.
Potential Uses: Coffee drinks, flavoring, cosmetics.

Crop Name: Sugarcane

Varieties: Isd-37, Isd-40, Co-0238.
Cultivation Method: Plant stem cuttings (setts).
Soil Requirement: Fertile loamy soil.
Harvesting Techniques: Cut mature stalks at the base.
Nutrition Value: Natural sugars, carbohydrates.
Potential Uses: Sugar, jaggery, molasses, ethanol.
