Crop Details

Rice Farming

Rice farming is the cultivation of rice in warm, fertile, and well-watered fields. It includes preparing the land, planting seeds or seedlings, providing proper irrigation and nutrients, controlling weeds and pests, and harvesting the mature crop to produce rice for food.

Wheat Farming

Wheat farming is the cultivation of wheat in cool, fertile, and well-drained soil. It involves preparing the land, sowing quality seeds, providing proper irrigation and nutrients, controlling weeds and pests, and harvesting the mature crop to produce wheat for food.

Corn Farming

Corn farming is the cultivation of corn (maize) in warm, fertile, and well-drained soil. It involves preparing the land, planting quality seeds, providing proper irrigation and nutrients, controlling weeds and pests, and harvesting the mature crop for food, animal feed, or industrial use.

Pest Control Strategies

Biological Control

Biological control uses natural enemies such as predators, parasites, and beneficial microorganisms to reduce pest populations. For example, ladybugs feed on aphids, while certain bacteria and fungi can kill harmful insects. This method is environmentally friendly and helps reduce the need for chemical pesticides.

Chemical Control

Chemical control involves the use of pesticides, insecticides, herbicides, or fungicides to manage pests, weeds, and diseases. These chemicals should be applied at the recommended dose and only when necessary to avoid harming beneficial organisms, preventing pesticide resistance, and reducing environmental pollution.

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is a sustainable approach that combines biological, cultural, mechanical, and chemical control methods. It focuses on regular pest monitoring, preventing infestations, and using chemical pesticides only as a last resort. IPM helps protect crops while minimizing risks to human health and the environment.
